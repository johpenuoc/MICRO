=== _OPERATORS ===
_ADD, for example, takes the last two items stored in the STACK and adds them.


=== _PUSH ===
This moves the last calculated item onto the STACK.


=== _CALL ===
This 'calls' a function. May return an answer that'll be pushed onto the STACK within the fuction.


=== _STO ===
This takes two arguments: VALUE : LOCATION. For example:
    
    STO STACK(-1), HASH(KEYL[-1])

    This will store the value from the end of STACK into the hashtable with the key at the last index stored in KEYL 'keylist'.
    
    
=== _LOAD ===
_LOAD retrievs from the hashtable a certain value. It takes one argument: the key for the hashtable.


=== _SET ===
This allocates space on the hashtable and updates the KEYL (keylist) with the key.


=== _PUSH_KEYL ===
This will push a key (which will just be a STRING) into the keylist.
